- **Phase 1 — Audit & Foundation**: Project architecture initialized with Vite 6 + React 19 + Tailwind CSS + Lucide React. Hot pink brand design tokens, centralized data architecture, state tracking, layout, Navbar, Footer, StickyMobileCta, and SEO assets.
- **Phase 2 — Homepage**: Full consumer-grade homepage with high-impact Hero focusing on signature hot-pink packaging, Trust Strip, interactive "Choose Your B-Care" catalog showcase, 6 verified feature cards ("Why Choose B-Care?"), Absorption demo video teaser, 3-path Customer Journey visual cards, and dual business partner CTAs ("Buy in Bulk" & "Become a Dealer").

Detailed ProductCard components with size/quantity badges, price placeholders, feature checklists, and marketplace trigger buttons. Comprehensive ProductDetailModal with specifications and wholesale shortcuts. Fit & coverage sizing guide.
- **Phase 4 — Demo + Trust**: Absorption demonstration video section (`/demo`, `/why-b-care`) with custom player interface, "What Are You Seeing?" contextual explanation without fake lab claims, "Made With Care" 100% pure cotton highlight, verified 3-layer construction breakdown, patented technology section, and founder spotlight for Poonam Sandip Bhamre (`/about`).
- **Phase 5 — Wholesale**: Dedicated commercial bulk-order section (`/wholesale-dealership`) with dealer contact card, pre-filled WhatsApp ordering flow, and full validated Wholesale Enquiry Form.
- **Phase 6 — Dealership**: Dedicated partnership section with "Bring B-Care to Your City" headline, 3-step visual onboarding process (Enquire -> Connect -> Discuss), comprehensive Dealer Application Form with distribution experience fields, and searchable extensible Dealer Directory architecture.
- **Phase 7 — Contact**: Complete contact center (`/contact`) with Poonam Sandip Bhamre ownership credentials, validated enquiry form, direct WhatsApp support trigger, and full 8-question FAQ accordion with exact verified answers.
- **Phase 8 — SEO + Accessibility + Performance**: Complete meta tags, Open Graph, sitemap.xml, robots.txt, SVG favicon, skip link (`#main-content`), ARIA labels, semantic markup, high-contrast typography, and `prefers-reduced-motion` compliance.
